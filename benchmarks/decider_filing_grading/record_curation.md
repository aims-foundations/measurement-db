# Decider Filing Grading curation record

- **Coverage.** All 575 recorded grades over the 179 corpus filings: Pass C and Pass R verdicts for every item, Iris remediation verdicts for 38 items, and the human-locked final for every item. Each response is one subject's verdict under the shared rubric (SIGNAL, NO SIGNAL, or ABSTAIN); traces preserve each grade's recorded basis text. The retracted 2026-10-14 round contributes no grades.
- **Curation decisions.** Verdicts are released as recorded, on a three-value nominal scale; abstentions remain their own category, never collapsed into verdicts. One on-disk Pass R row (accession 0001628280-26-065570, SIGNAL) was superseded at finals v2 by a recorded substitution (the second operator's own-session ABSTAIN under the protocol's substitution rule); the released row and the locked final are both preserved as recorded. Item content names the filing and the form and filer facts stated in the locked grade's basis; full filing text remains on EDGAR.
- **Limitations.** The corpus is mixed-scope: the 38 remediation items rest on complete-submission packets, while the other 141 rest on the earlier extraction scope (primary document plus EX-99 exhibits, 140,000-character cap). Packet assembly used an October 14, 2026 extraction builder, and batch sampling used that round's labels for strata only; graders never saw them, and that round's quarantined labels were excluded from grading.
